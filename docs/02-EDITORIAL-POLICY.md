# LambdaMatters Editorial Policy & Boundaries

This document defines what belongs on LambdaMatters, the operational boundaries between LambdaMatters and personal websites, and the editorial invariants governing all published content.

---

## 1. The Division of Labor

To prevent taxonomy paralysis and creative friction, the boundary between LambdaMatters and personal sites is absolute:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│            LambdaMatters             │     │      Personal Sites (e.g. vijayanant) │
│         (lambdamatters.com)          │     │          (vijayanant.com)            │
│                                      │     │                                      │
│  • Formal Technical Reports (PDFs)   │     │  • Narrative Essays & War Stories    │
│  • Empirical Benchmarks & Datasets   │     │  • Origin Stories & Personal Journey │
│  • Production-Grade Open Source Repos│     │  • Tutorials & Architectural Opinion │
│  • Canonical System Specifications   │     │  • Personal Career & Availability    │
│  • Shared Joint Talks & Algorithms   │     │  • Chronological Blog Archive        │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘     └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. The Zero-Blogging Rule

**LambdaMatters does not host personal blogs, tutorials, or informal opinion pieces.**

### The Rule
* All essays, retrospectives, tutorials, and craft opinions are published on **`vijayanant.com`** or Raghu's personal platform.
* LambdaMatters serves solely as an **exhibition gallery and canonical release hub**.
* If a founder writes a personal deep-dive about a LambdaMatters project (such as *"The Ammunition Effect: Why Giving an AI the Whole Document Degrades Code Review"*), that post lives on their personal blog. LambdaMatters includes a secondary link:  
  * *"Author's Essay &rarr; [vijayanant.com]"*

---

## 3. Project Admission Criteria

Nothing is featured on the LambdaMatters front page unless it meets the **Triple Verification Standard**:

1. **Working Code or Verifiable Artifact:** Every project card must point to a real repository, reproducible code, or downloadable paper. No vaporware.
2. **Empirical or Mathematical Grounding:** Claims must be accompanied by reproducible data (e.g. -94.6% context reduction, 1000x extrapolation retention, or prime modulo proofs).
3. **Stand-Alone Permanence:** Artifacts must remain valuable and readable independent of temporary industry trends or product cycles.

---

## 4. Attribution & Authorship Law

LambdaMatters is a partnership of two independent thinkers, not an anonymous corporation.

* **Explicit Attribution:** Every report, project, and algorithm explicitly names the primary author(s).
  * *Report 01 (Compiled Intent):* Vijay Anant
  * *Report 02 (Newton Reborn):* Raghu Ugare
  * *AutoCombo & Functional Conf Talks:* Vijay Anant & Raghu Ugare
* **The Author Bridge:** Author names must always link directly to the founder's canonical personal website. 

---

## 5. Style & Tone Standards

All prose published on LambdaMatters must adhere to these editorial standards:

* **Factual & Measured:** State the problem, the methodology, and the measured outcome. Let the numbers and diagrams demonstrate superiority.
* **No Marketing Adjectives:** Words like "revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive", "cutting-edge", and "world-class" are strictly banned.
* **Punctuation Standards:**
  * Strictly **zero em-dashes** across all markup, titles, and text content. Use commas, colons, or parentheses instead.
  * Clean typographic quotes and standardized mathematical symbols.
