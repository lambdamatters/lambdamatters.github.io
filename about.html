About the Studio

A Decade of Intellectual Collaboration

LambdaMatters is an independent systems research studio founded by Vijay Anant and Raghu Ugare. It is the shared intellectual home where we develop and publish original systems, protocols, and technical investigations from first principles.

We do not operate as an agency, dev shop, or marketing consultancy. We are veteran software engineers and applied mathematicians whose work speaks through running code, formal technical reports, and verifiable empirical data.
